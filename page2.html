Rosé

Roseanne Park, also known as Rosé, is a South Korean-New Zealand singer and songwriter best known as a member of the popular K-pop girl group BLACKPINK. She was born on February 11, 1997, in Auckland, New Zealand, and grew up in Melbourne, Australia. Rosé debuted with BLACKPINK in August 2016 under YG Entertainment, where she quickly gained recognition for her unique voice, dancing skills, and sweet personality. Rosé has also built a successful solo career alongside her journey with BLACKPINK. In March 2021, she made her solo debut with her first single album, R, which featured the songs On The Ground and Gone. Her solo music allowed her to express her own musical style and showcase her emotional vocals and songwriting abilities. In December 2024, she released her first full-length solo album, rosie, which included songs such as Number One Girl, Toxic Till the End, and APT., her popular collaboration with Bruno Mars. The song APT. became a worldwide hit, gaining millions of streams and introducing her music to an even wider audience. Through her solo career, Rosé has also gained more creative freedom, allowing her to explore different genres and share more personal experiences through her music. Her album rosie reflects her emotions, relationships, and personal growth, making it especially meaningful to her and her fans.
